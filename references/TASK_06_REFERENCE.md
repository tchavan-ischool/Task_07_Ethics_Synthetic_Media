# ElevenLabs Experiment

**Tool:** ElevenLabs  
**Voice:** Siren — Natural Realistic Conversational Voice

Two synthetic audio versions were generated.

Version 1 was selected as the stronger final artifact.

The primary synthetic characteristic identified during human evaluation was limited natural breathing between ideas.

---

# Vidnoz Experiment

**Tool:** Vidnoz AI  
**Avatar:** Annie  
**Voice:** Annie (Lifelike)

Two synthetic-avatar videos were generated.

Version 1 was selected as the stronger artifact.

The main weaknesses involved limited facial expression and robotic vocal emotion.

Version 2 used shorter sentences and stronger pauses but appeared less realistic because the pauses disrupted natural pacing.

# Detection Experiment

ElevenLabs Version 1 was evaluated using AI Voice Detector.

The artifact was known to be completely synthetic.

The detector returned:

**AI Score:** 16%

**Verdict:** Likely Human

**Segments analyzed:** 19

**Maximum AI score:** 50%

Only one segment was classified as "Likely AI."

The result therefore represented a false negative.

---

# Relevance to Task 07

This result became the central practical observation behind Task 7.

It demonstrates that automated detection cannot always reliably establish whether an artifact is synthetic.

Task 7 therefore examines alternative and complementary safeguards including:

- disclosure
- consent
- provenance
- factual verification
- human review
- organizational governance
- incident response
- refusal authority

---

# Core Lesson

The connection between the two projects can be summarized as:

> **Task 6 demonstrated the technical problem. Task 7 develops an organizational response to that problem.**

The technical experiment showed that synthetic media can appear realistic enough to challenge automated detection.

The ethical and governance analysis therefore focuses on creating safeguards that do not depend on detection alone.
